About

I am a Master of Research student at Southern Cross University and a Research Assistant at UNSW, Australia.

My research focuses on trustworthy machine learning, with particular interests in anomaly detection, distribution shift, out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization, uncertainty quantification, and adversarial robustness.

I am currently investigating the reliability of machine learning models in safety-critical battery systems, where distribution shifts, limited fault data, and deployment constraints present significant challenges. More broadly, I am interested in reliable and generalizable AI methods for real-world scientific and engineering applications.

Current Research

My current research investigates trustworthy machine learning for battery anomaly detection, with an emphasis on reliability under real-world operating conditions.

Key challenges of interest include distribution shifts across operating conditions and battery systems, limited fault data, uncertainty in anomaly predictions, and the gap between benchmark performance and deployment reliability. I am particularly interested in moving beyond predictive accuracy towards reliable risk assessment and decision support in safety-critical energy applications.
