About

I am a Master of Research student at Southern Cross University and a Research Assistant at UNSW. My research focuses on trustworthy artificial intelligence for safety-critical battery systems.

I am particularly interested in understanding when and why AI models fail beyond their training distribution, including under natural distribution shifts and adversarial perturbations, and in developing reliable and secure AI methods for battery health, anomaly detection, and energy systems.

Research Interests

  • Trustworthy AI for Safety-Critical Battery Systems
  • Out-of-Distribution Generalization
  • Adversarial Robustness and AI Security
  • Battery Health and Anomaly Detection
  • Reliable Machine Learning
  • AI for Energy Systems

Current Research

I am currently investigating the reliability of battery AI models beyond their training distribution, with particular emphasis on natural distribution shifts and physically plausible adversarial perturbations. My long-term goal is to develop trustworthy, generalizable, and secure AI methods for real-world battery management systems.
